## Full Description
Welcome to **Royal Spades: Cobweb & Velvet**, the definitive 4-player partnership Spades experience designed from the ground up for pure strategic depth, fluid mobile play, and zero ads or paywalls.

### Key Features
- **Official Partnership Rules**: 4 players, 2 teams (You & North vs East & West). 13 tricks per deal, accurate bidding, Nil bids (±100 pts), spades broken rule, and sandbag penalties.
- **2's Wild Option**: Activate the thrilling Deuces Wild variant! All four 2s are crowned as supreme trumps:
  * 👑 **2 of Hearts (2♥)**: The Supreme Trump
  * ⚔️ **2 of Clubs (2♣)**: Second Highest Trump
  * 💎 **2 of Diamonds (2♦)**: Third Highest Trump
  * ♠️ **2 of Spades (2♠)**: Fourth Highest Trump
  * Followed by Ace of Spades (A♠), King of Spades (K♠), and down to 3♠!
- **Intelligent AI**: Smart partner and opponent bots that coordinate bids, protect your Nil bids, attack opponent leads, and count cards.
- **Offline & Battery Friendly**: Zero internet required. Lightweight procedural Web Audio sound effects.
- **100% Ad-Free**: No video ads, no popups, no tracking.
