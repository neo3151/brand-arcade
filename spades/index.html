Rules of Royal Spades

Partnerships: You and North (Us) play together against East and West (Them).

Bidding: Each player bids the tricks they expect to take (0 to 13). Team bid is the combined total. Bidding Nil (0) awards +100 pts if made, or -100 pts if you take any trick!

Must Follow Suit: You must play a card of the led suit if you have one. If void, you may play any card (including Spades / Trumps).

Spades Broken: Spades cannot be led until someone cuts with a spade on an off-suit trick ("spades broken"), or you have only spades in hand.

Sandbags: Every trick taken over your team bid gives 1 bag. Accumulating 10 bags incurs a -100 point penalty!

👑 2's Wild Hierarchy:

When 2's Wild is enabled, all four 2s act as top trump cards in this exact order:

2♥ (Supreme) > 2♣ > 2♦ > 2♠ > A♠ > K♠...
