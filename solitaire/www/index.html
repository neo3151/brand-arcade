Rules of the Garden

Objective: Build four Royal Foundations from Ace to King for each suit (♠, ♥, ♦, ♣).

Tableau Building: Stack cards in descending order with alternating colors (e.g., Red 7 upon Black 8). Only Kings may move to open lanes.

Touch & Drag: Drag stacks smoothly or simply tap any card to auto-send it to its best valid place.

Foundation Play: You can also drag or tap cards from the foundations back down to the table if needed.

Power-Ups (No Ads):

  • Looking Glass Hint: Reveals the smartest strategic move and explains why (free guidance for deck draws).
  • Cheshire Peek: Flips a face-down card temporarily to reveal what lies ahead.
  • Wonderland Reshuffle: Reshuffles the pocket deck when you find yourself in a corner (fully undoable).
