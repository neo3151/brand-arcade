TELEPRINTER FIELD PROTOCOL

1. OBJECTIVE: The teleprinter holds a hidden secret cipher sequence of colored pins. Your mission is to guess the exact combination before all transmission attempts are exhausted.

2. CLUE EVALUATION (FEEDBACK PEGS): After transmitting each guess row, the cipher unit stamps feedback pegs on the right:

  • 🔴 RED PEG: One of your pins is the correct color in the correct position.
  • 🟠 AMBER PEG: One of your pins is the correct color, but in the wrong position.
  • ⚫ EMPTY PEG: A pin color that does not match any remaining secret pins.

Note: The order of feedback pegs does not correspond to the pin order in your row. Duplicate pins in the secret code are accounted for strictly one-to-one.

3. TACTICAL FIELD SUPPLIES:

  • Signal Decryptor: Decodes and permanently locks 1 secret pin compartment.
  • Frequency Filter: Identifies and disables 2 unused colors on your palette.
  • Magnetic Tape Rewind: Spools back the tape to give you +2 extra guess attempts.
  • Wiretap Logic Probe: Verifies if your pending test row mathematically conforms to all previous clues.
