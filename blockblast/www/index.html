How to Play

1. Place Polyominoes: Drag shapes from the bottom tray onto the 8×8 grid, or tap a shape then tap any grid cell.
2. Blast Lines: Completely fill any row or column to clear it. Rows and columns clear simultaneously!
3. Build Combos: Clearing lines on consecutive placements builds a massive combo multiplier.
4. Don't Get Trapped: If no remaining pieces in your tray can fit on the board, the run ends! Use Hammers and Rotations to rescue tight spots.
