## Short Description

## Full Description
Welcome to **Timber Block Blast**, a pure, ad-free mobile puzzle experience!

Drop handcrafted wooden polyomino blocks onto the 8x8 grid. Clear horizontal and vertical lines simultaneously, unleash dazzling blast animations, and build up escalating combo multipliers for massive high scores.

### Key Features:
- **Relaxing Wood Grain Aesthetics**: Warm timber felt, tactile sound effects, and clean animations.
- **Dual Controls**: Smooth drag-and-drop with mobile finger offset preview + intuitive Tap-to-Place.
- **Combos & Super Blasts**: Clear consecutive lines to trigger multi-tone musical chords and combo multipliers.
- **Power-ups & Rescues**: Use Hammer Bombs to shatter 3x3 blocks, Rotations to pivot tricky pieces, and Rerolls to deal fresh shapes.
- **Continue Anytime**: Automatic state persistence ensures your run is safely preserved if you step away.
- **Themes & Powerup Shop**: Collect coins by clearing lines to unlock vibrant themes including Neon Cyber, Royal Jewels, and Pastel Slate.
- **Zero Ads & Zero In-App Purchases**: Pure, offline, uninterrupted gameplay.

### Target Audience
Casual puzzle enthusiasts, fans of Block Blast, Woodoku, 1010!, and spatial logic games of all ages.
