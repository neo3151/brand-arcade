THE PATH OF HARMONY // RULES

1. OBJECTIVE: Clear all 144 carved jade and bone tiles from the sacred formation by matching open pairs.

2. MATCHING PRINCIPLES:

  • Suits & Honors (Wan, Bamboo, Dots, Winds, Dragons): Must match exactly in both suit and value (e.g. 5-Bamboo matches 5-Bamboo; East Wind matches East Wind).
  • Seasons (🌸 Spring, ☀️ Summer, 🍁 Autumn, ❄️ Winter): Any Season tile matches with any other Season tile!
  • Flowers (🌺 Plum, 🌿 Orchid, 🌼 Chrysanthemum, 🎋 Bamboo): Any Flower tile matches with any other Flower tile!

3. FREE TILE CONDITIONS: A tile is selectable only when:

  • No tile is resting directly on top of it on a higher layer.
  • At least one lateral side (left or right) is open and unblocked.

Tip: Free tiles are bright and interactive. Blocked tiles are dimmed to guide your vision effortlessly.

4. DAILY SUPPLY:

  • Compass Hint (🧭): Illuminates an open matching pair with a vibrant jade pulse.
  • Dragon Shuffle (🐉): Rearranges remaining tiles to open matching paths.
  • Time Rewind (⏳): Reverses the last match and restores both tiles to the board.
  • Celestial Lotus (🪷): Banishes any chosen free tile and its open matching twin (or any open pair).
